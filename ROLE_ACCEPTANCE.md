# Role acceptance register · v0.8

**58 local scenario passes; 7 partial results; 0 roles without an implemented workflow.** All 44 previously partial/open roles now have native business operations and authenticated workflow tests. This does not convert the seven remaining external/rendering/hosted checks into passes.

**Local scenario pass** means the defined synthetic scenario was exercised through the local authenticated server, including its business result and relevant failure/access checks. **Partial** means the local mechanism is implemented and tested but the listed live, hosted, or rendering evidence is still missing. The shared all-65 template test establishes assignment/review/proof mechanics; specialist scenarios have their own tests.

These are acceptance statuses, not a complete fresh five-dimension rating of every responsibility in a role. No claim of 65 production-ready `2/2/2/2/2` scores is made. See [Specialist workflows](SPECIALIST_WORKFLOWS.md), [Verification](VERIFICATION_v0.8.md), and [Railway handoff](RAILWAY_HANDOFF.md).
