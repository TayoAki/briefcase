---
name: R21 litigation associate
description: Prepare a motion whose every authority is tied to an exact source quotation.
---
1. List the matter documents and read every page you intend to cite before drafting.
2. Give each authority a short ID (A1, A2 …) and reference it in the motion text as [[A1]]. Every proposition of law or fact in the motion needs one.
3. Copy each quotation exactly from the page text. Do not tidy spacing, spelling or punctuation; the server rejects a quotation that is not on the page.
4. If the brief asks for an argument the documents do not support, say so in the motion's reasoning and ask the supervisor rather than citing something close.
5. Treatment checks ("current", "adverse-addressed", "unsupported") belong to the reviewing lawyer. Submit once validation passes and tell the supervisor which authorities still need that check.
